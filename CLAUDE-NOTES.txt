# Hatch Research - Claude Notes

## Project Overview
Stock research web app for NASDAQ & NYSE shares (traded via Hatch platform NZ). Scores stocks on fundamentals weighted by research-backed predictive strength. Supports manual ticker lookup, side-by-side comparison, and full exchange scanning.

## Scoring System (100 points max)
Weighted by research-backed predictive strength:
- **Business Quality (35 pts)**: FCF margin (12), ROE (12), revenue growth (8), profit margin (3)
- **Valuation (25 pts)**: PEG (10), forward P/E (8), P/B (4), trailing P/E (3)
- **Financial Strength (20 pts)**: D/E (8), current ratio (6), FCF positive (6)
- **Market Sentiment (20 pts)**: analyst buy% (8), price vs target (6), 52-wk position (6)
- Dividends, beta, payout ratio shown but not scored (weak predictors)

## Hatch Eligibility Filters
- Market cap > $300M USD
- Average daily volume > 100k shares
- Listed on NASDAQ or NYSE

## Data Sources
- yfinance (Yahoo Finance): all fundamentals, analyst data, price data. No API key.
- Finnhub (optional): full exchange ticker lists. Free key, 60 req/min, no daily cap.

## Architecture
- Flask web app on port 5001
- Background threading for exchange scans
- JSON file caching (24-hour validity)
- Dark theme matching other projects
